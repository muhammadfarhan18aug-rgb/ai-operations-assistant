# Procurement Policy

## Purpose
The company approves purchases through a valid purchase order and recorded business need before any vendor commitment is made.

## Approval threshold
All purchase requests above $5,000 require manager approval and a signed vendor quote before the purchase order is created.

## Required review
Employees must confirm the requested item, supplier, quantity, and budget before the procurement request is submitted.
