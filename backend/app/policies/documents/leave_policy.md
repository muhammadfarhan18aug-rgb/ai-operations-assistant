# Leave Policy

## Annual leave entitlement
Full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Part-time entitlement is prorated by contracted hours.

## Request notice
Submit annual leave requests in the HR system at least 10 working days before the first day requested. A manager must approve the request before leave is booked.

## Carry-over
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried days must be used by 31 March or they expire, except where local law requires otherwise.

## Sick leave
Notify your manager before the start of the workday on the first day of illness. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.
