# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submission deadline
Submit expense claims with itemized receipts within 30 calendar days of the transaction. Claims submitted after 30 days require written approval from Finance.

## Approval limits
A manager must approve any single expense above USD 250 before purchase. Expenses above USD 1,000 also require Finance approval.

## Travel and meals
Meals during approved business travel are reimbursable up to USD 60 per person per day. Alcohol, personal entertainment, and expenses without a business purpose are not reimbursable.

## Reimbursement timing
Approved and complete claims are paid in the next monthly reimbursement cycle. Missing receipts or business-purpose details return the claim to the requester for correction.
